# Layouts, Templates, and Themes

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Specify how users choose and reuse slide layouts, reusable slides, templates, and visual themes in the first release. Decide which elements provide structure, starting content, or styling; whether a template can combine these; where user-created items live; and what built-in starter set is needed. Preserve the agreed distinction between a theme and a template and keep reusable content private to its owner.

## Answer

New-deck creation offers a blank deck and two built-in deck templates: a teaching deck and a C-level presentation deck. A template is a deck-level starting point, even when it contains only one slide. It may combine starting content and slide structure, and may provide slide backgrounds. Templates and themes remain independent: a user can choose or change a theme without changing the template's structure, content, or backgrounds.

The built-in standalone slide layouts are no layout, title, top image, left image, bottom image, right image, two column, full image, and list or bullet. Smart layouts are structured, editable slide objects inspired by Office SmartArt, with built-in forms such as timelines, columns and grids, funnels, galleries, and feature cards. Users edit the object's items and it reflows to fit them.

A reusable slide is a populated slide with actual content that can be inserted into another deck. On insertion, users can keep the slide's original styling or match the destination deck's theme; preserving its original styling is the default.

Themes apply across the deck and control colors and typography only. Backgrounds belong to templates. Decorative theme assets, including Sage's, are not applied automatically; they may be used explicitly or supplied by a template. Sage is the default theme. The initial theme choices also include Nebulae, Lux, Clementa, Lunaria, Night Sky, Peach, Blues, Flamingo, and Finesse.

User-created slide layouts, reusable slides, templates, and themes live in a private library available across the owner's projects. They remain private to their owner.
