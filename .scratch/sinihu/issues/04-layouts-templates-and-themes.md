# Layouts, Templates, and Themes

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Specify how users choose and reuse slide layouts, reusable slides, templates, and visual themes in the first release. Decide which elements provide structure, starting content, or styling; whether a template can combine these; where user-created items live; and what built-in starter set is needed. Preserve the agreed distinction between a theme and a template and keep reusable content private to its owner.
